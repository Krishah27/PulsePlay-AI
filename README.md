# 🏏 PulsePlay AI

### Watch Cricket With AI Personalities

PulsePlay AI is an autonomous AI-powered second-screen cricket experience that reacts to every ball in real time with dynamic personalities, emotional commentary, tactical insights, momentum analysis, and autonomous fan engagement powered by Gemini AI.

🚀 **Live Demo:**  
https://pulseplay-ai.netlify.app/

---

# ✨ What Makes PulsePlay AI Unique?

Most cricket apps only show scores.

PulsePlay AI experiences the match.

Instead of static dashboards, PulsePlay AI acts like a live AI companion that:
- reacts emotionally to every delivery
- changes personality dynamically
- generates tactical cricket insights
- creates fan engagement autonomously
- builds live match narratives in real time

Every fan watches cricket differently.

PulsePlay AI lets users choose the personality that watches the game alongside them.
